Tomi for iPhone, design round 3

The tasting menu, finished: every screen, dark theme, handoff

This round completes direction B. With rounds 1 and 2, every screen of the app now has a mockup: the lesson player from intro to celebration, the scene kit, Ask Tomi, hands-free voice, skill detail, profile, settings and the edge states. Phones are shown three to a row with notes underneath. A red dashed line marks the fold at 844 pt on any screen that scrolls. Six screens are repeated in the dark theme, and the last section is the token and component handoff for SwiftUI.

  • Onboarding is nine screens. The notification question now appears at the first timer (1c). The cupboard question now appears in the first scan review (7a).
  • Unsure ingredients are not counted until confirmed. They have their own dashed box with "I have it" and "No" (7a, D4).
  • A "never" cuisine is hidden everywhere, including "Also on tonight" (D1 shows one alternative, not two).
  • Rank ladder proposed: Novice, Commis, Cook, Saucier, Sous, Chef, Patron, Toque, at 150 XP a rank (6a).

Before building

Open questions and recommendations

Open questions

  • Rank names. Sous, Patron and Toque are shortened or informal so they fit the column. If the full words matter more (Sous chef, Chef patron), the stats column needs a two-line value.
  • XP numbers. I used 40 XP a dish and 10 for the quiz, and 150 XP a rank. At those rates twelve stamps is about 1,200 XP, just past Toque. The real numbers live in the rules engine.
  • Wrong quiz answers. Drawn as costing nothing and returning later. If a retry on the spot is wanted, the wrong state needs a "Try again" button.
  • "I stopped early". I assumed it keeps the streak only if a step was reached; that rule is not decided.
  • Thumbs down. The follow-up field is described, not drawn.
  • Hands-free and timers together. The listening bar replaces the footer, so Next and Back are voice or swipe only while it is on. That may be too strict; a slim Next button could stay.
  • Sample cooks differ between rounds. Round 2 followed a vegetarian on day one; this round returns to round 1's cook (chicken, level 2). The final build needs one consistent demo account.

Not covered, or thin

  • Motion is described, not shown. The stamp press, the line being inked and the timer rule draining need a prototype in SwiftUI to judge.
  • Dark theme covers six screens. The camera, celebration, profile and settings screens were not drawn in dark; they use the same tokens.
  • Scenes are static. Fifteen are drawn. Slice, mince, peel, grate, measure, pat dry, scramble, fry egg, blanch, fluff, toss pasta and taste are still to draw from the same parts.
  • Dynamic Type and long translations were not tested. Dish lines and the stats column are the tightest places.
  • Reset, the iOS permission dialogs and the keyboard-up states are not drawn.

Recommendation

Build the lesson player first, with the dish line and stamp as its reward.

Why. It is where the cook spends the evening and it uses nearly every component: progress rule, plate, step text, timer block, tip, footer, sheet, tick rows and the stats columns. Once it works, home, skill detail and profile are mostly dish lines and rules.

Lock the tokens before any screen. Light and dark share every role, so the palette in section 9 can go in as one asset catalogue. Tomi and the scenes should be code-drawn from the same part list so the print offset and the dark outline are parameters, not second drawings.

Prototype three motions early: the stamp press, the dish line writing itself in, and the red timer rule. They carry the reward in a style that has no confetti, and they are the part these stills cannot prove.
