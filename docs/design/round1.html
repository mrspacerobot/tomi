Tomi for iPhone, design round 1

Three identities that come from the kitchen

The prototype borrowed its look from Duolingo. Every direction below drops the five traits that made it read that way and keeps what is Tomi's own: the tomato chef, the warm tone, and the game rules (XP, a nightly streak, two cooks to master a skill, prerequisites, one skill that is next up). Shapes now come from the kitchen: a room with stations, a printed menu, a box of recipe cards. Buttons are flat and sentence case. Streak, level and XP are shown as kitchen objects or plain type. Tomi is redrawn with arms, a tilted toque, leaves and four moods, in three treatments.

All three show the same progress and follow the real unlock rules: Knife basics, Eggs 101 and Searing are mastered, Boil & blanch is at 1 of 2 cooks, Pan sauces is next up. Sauté, Roasting and Emulsions are ready because their only prerequisite, Knife basics, is done. Rice, Pasta, Braising and Balancing flavor are locked.

ATomi's kitchen

Home is a room. The skills are the things in it, and they light up as you earn them.

Build
Highest. About 14 code-drawn objects, each lit and unlit, placed by hand with custom hit areas.
Past 12 skills
Weak. Skill 13 needs a new object and a spot in the room. Fitting 12 with readable labels already took two walls.
What is next
Medium. The lit burner and flag mark it, but that is a scroll away, so the note by the fridge has to say it too.

BTasting menu

The skill path is a printed menu: four courses, twelve dishes, a chef's stamp when you master one.

Build
Lowest. A list with custom rows. The only drawing is the stamp, the pen circle and the print-style Tomi.
Past 12 skills
Best. A new skill is a new line, a new unit is a new course. 40 skills would still read.
What is next
High. The special sits at the top and the same dish is circled on the card below.

CRecipe box

Every lesson you cook becomes a card you keep. Skills are the tabs, and the box fills up.

Build
Medium. An overlapping card stack, one tab shape, a pulled-out state and small rotations. Standard layout, little drawing.
Past 12 skills
Good. A skill is one more card behind a divider. Past about 30, dividers need to collapse, and the recipe collection will want search.
What is next
High as drawn: the pulled card and tonight's card agree. In a much longer box the pulled card can scroll out of view.
A

Tomi's kitchen

A cut-paper kitchen in enamel colours. The butter-yellow fridge is the scan button, tonight's lesson is a note taped beside it, and every pan, pot and jar is a skill. Unlit objects are locked, the burner that is on is tonight's.

Map

  • Stations, not nodes. Board, top shelf, pot shelf, four burners, two ovens. A skill is the object plus a shelf-edge label.
  • State is drawn three ways: full colour or grey silhouette, a label (Mastered, 1 of 2 cooks, Ready, Needs Boil), and pips or a lock.
  • Next up is the only burner with a flame and a turned knob.

Game layer

  • Streak is a tear-off calendar, XP is a measuring jug filling toward the next level. Two objects with plain text, no pill row.
  • Scan is the fridge itself. The whole door is the touch target.

Open questions

  • The scene is two walls stacked with a strip of floor between them. It reads as a doll's house, which may or may not be wanted.
  • Labels at 13 / 11.5 pt are the smallest text in the three directions.

B

Tasting menu

Cobalt ink on white paper with a red rubber stamp, like a bistro carte. The path is typeset, not illustrated. Tonight's lesson is the special, the next skill is circled in pen, and Tomi is drawn as a three-ink print.

Map

  • Courses, not units. A course heading is small type and a rule, never a coloured banner.
  • Dish line: name, dotted leader, state in words. Mastered adds Tomi's stamp, in progress shows two diamonds, locked shows a lock and what it needs.
  • Next up is circled by hand in red and tagged as tonight's special.

Game layer

  • Streak, rank and XP are three columns of type under the masthead, like the head of a bill. Levels borrow kitchen ranks: level 2 is Commis.
  • Scan is the second button on the special. Before the first scan of the evening it becomes the primary one.

Open questions

  • It is the least playful of the three. The stamp and the pen marks carry all of the warmth.
  • Serif body text is a risk at arm's length from a stove. The step screen sets it at 22 pt to compensate.

C

Recipe box

Index cards, tab dividers and Tomi's handwriting, in a teal tin. Each skill card lists the dishes you have cooked for it, so progress is a collection you can cook from again. The next skill is the card pulled halfway out.

Map

  • Cards behind dividers. Dividers are the four units. Tabs stagger left, middle, right like a real card file.
  • State: a mastered card lists its two recipes and carries a tick sticker, in progress shows one recipe and one empty slot, locked cards are kraft with a lock and what they need.
  • Next up is pulled out of the stack with tonight's recipe pencilled in.

Game layer

  • Cook's log card: seven day boxes ticked by hand for the streak, and a pencil line for XP to the next level.
  • Scan is the fridge snapshot clipped to tonight's card, plus a labelled button.

Open questions

  • The home screen is the longest of the three. A collapsed box (dividers only) may be the right default.
  • Handwriting is used for Tomi's voice only and never below 19 pt, but it still needs a legibility check on device.

Recommendation

Build C, the recipe box, and borrow the fridge from A.

Why C. It is the only direction where the reward is something real. After each lesson you own a recipe card, and the skill map is simply where those cards live. That matches the promise (learn to cook from what is in your fridge) better than lighting up an object or stamping a line, and it gives people a reason to open the app on nights they are not learning anything new.

Why not the others. A is the most charming picture and the most expensive to keep true: every new skill is an illustration and a layout problem, and the map is already two screens of scenery for twelve skills. B is the safest to build and the clearest list, but it moves Tomi out of the kitchen into a dining room and has the thinnest game feel.

What to borrow. From A, the fridge as a drawn object for the scan entry point and for the scan flow itself, and the cut-paper kitchen for celebration screens. From B, the discipline of the dish line: name, leader, state in words. Round 2 would take the chosen direction through scan, chips, quiz, celebration and Ask Tomi, in light and dark.
