Cooking Show, every screen.

The whole app in the Cooking Show look: curtains, a lit sign, a stage and spotlight, cream cards and one red button. 37 screens in five parts, from sign-in to what happens when you are offline.

Feel: haptics that match the animation

Like Duolingo, each big moment pairs a small vibration with the motion on screen, so a right answer or a finished dish is felt as well as seen. A web page cannot vibrate a phone, so this is the plan rather than a demo.

MomentOn screenFeel
Pick an answer or a chipThe card pops and gets its tickOne light tick (selection)
Quiz: correctTomi cheers, the answer glows greenSuccess pattern
Quiz: not quiteThe card wobbles onceOne soft bump, never harsh
Commercial break endsThe TV flashes "Time!"Repeating taps until you look, alongside the sound and the screen
That's a wrapCurtain call and confettiA short rising roll, timed to the confetti burst
XP counting upThe score ticks upA light tick per step, fading out
New level unlockedThe curtain opens on the new levelOne firm thump as it opens
  • SwiftUI's sensoryFeedback covers the simple ones with no UIKit. The custom rolls need Core Haptics, which would sit behind a service like voice and camera.
  • Each vibration is fired from the same animation timeline as Tomi's motion, so the two stay in step.
  • It follows the phone's System Haptics setting, and Reduce Motion keeps the haptic while the motion stops.
  • Never the only signal: every moment is also on screen.
